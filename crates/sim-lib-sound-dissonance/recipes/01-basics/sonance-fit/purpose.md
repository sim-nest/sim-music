# Bounded Sonance Fit (descriptor)

Documents the locked sonance fitting configuration. The Rust API runs brute-force,
coordinate, and bounded stochastic parameter searches through
`sim-lib-discrete-search::SearchControl`, separates training, validation, and
locked conformance fixtures, and reports corpus hashes, licenses, objective
components, termination, seed, and digest. Tuned candidates are published as
evidence and do not replace the built-in roughness defaults.
