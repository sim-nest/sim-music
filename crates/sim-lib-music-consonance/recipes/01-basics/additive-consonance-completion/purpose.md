# Reversible Additive Consonance Completion

Documents the bounded Rust completion surface exercised by the checked crate
specimen. The completion search reuses `sim-lib-discrete-search`, never mutates
or rewrites source material, and keeps partial or cancelled termination status
honest when a feasible patch was emitted before the bound.

The returned patch records typed additions and the canonical content identity of
its exact source staff. Its inverse checks the introduced values before removal
and proves exact restoration of voice, note, and event identities.
