Builds recursive music catalogs without hiding recursion or inventing a
domain-specific traversal engine. The alphabet distinguishes variables from
constants, every variable has a productive fallback, contextual productions
override their fallback, and conservative generation bounds are checked before
search starts.

The scale states carry their scale-following pitch maps as data. Progression
trees declare a finite preorder topology and value catalog, so the shared
mixed-radix rank adapter supplies stable identities. Filtered exploration runs
through the shared search engine with explicit work, result, frontier, memory,
and seed evidence. Results retain derivation trees, production counts, ranks,
policy digests, pitch-map program digests, and search receipts.
