# Certified optimal voice leading (descriptor)

Documents the checked exact-identity fixture in which sequential greedy
selection costs 100 while the generic discrete assignment owner finds and
certifies cost 82. Voice, note, and event identities survive the projection
from score boundaries through motion records; unequal counts use explicit
entrance, departure, or doubling operations; equal optima replay in a stable
order. Adjacent assignments compose into a path certificate.

The same exact progression surface provides duration multiplication, overlay,
half-open slicing, and repetition. Repetition retains the first occurrence's
identities and reports every derived note/event identity thereafter.

Music execution is outside the cookbook sandbox, so this recipe is a descriptor;
the crate's checked Rust fixture performs the greedy/optimal comparison and
certificate verification.
