# Sequence Transform (descriptor)

Documents the exact score workbench: loss-audited conversion among melody, chord, staff, counterpoint, piano roll, snapshot, change stream, and progression forms; identity-preserving sustain, slur, expansion, delayed-note separation, and register unwrapping; and the exact duration, sequence, parallel, slice, and rhythm-mask laws. It also retains pitch-map policies for explicit holes, nudges, rejections, and inverse witnesses. Music sequencing, synthesis, and notation run through the audio and render pipeline outside the cookbook sandbox eval stack, so this is documented rather than run in the cookbook, whose sandbox eval stack loads only core construction and math over the number domains.
