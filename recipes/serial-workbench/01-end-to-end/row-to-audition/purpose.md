This recipe treats serial composition as a reusable workbench instead of a
closed application. It loads a realizer by stable id, freezes one immutable row
plan, proves a strict chromatic rendering, lands the same plan onto a modal
spine, completes the modal result with one reversible additive patch, audits the
structural and sounding ledgers, reverses the patch, exports MIDI plus bounded
LilyPond notation, and lowers an audition score.
